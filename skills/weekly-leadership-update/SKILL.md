# Weekly Leadership Update

## Purpose

Produce a concise, evidence-backed leadership update that separates proven value from active work and makes risks, decisions, owners, and next milestones easy to find.

## Required inputs

Ask for any missing items before drafting:

- Intended audience
- Reporting period
- Priority areas or commitments
- Approved source materials
- Desired length or template
- Decisions or actions the update should support
- Any sensitive topics, exclusions, or approval requirements

## Procedure

1. Review all approved sources and list what is available.
2. Extract factual updates, dates, owners, metrics, decisions, risks, dependencies, and next milestones.
3. Remove duplicates and reconcile repeated items.
4. Separate the content into:
   - Reported, validated, or realized results
   - Active work or pilots
   - Risks and blockers
   - Decisions needed
   - Next milestones
5. Distinguish verified facts from interpretation, estimates, targets, and open questions.
6. Identify missing evidence or conflicting information.
7. Draft the update using the format in `references/output-template.md` or the user’s supplied template.
8. Keep the TL;DR to five lines maximum.
9. Make each leadership ask explicit: decision, why it is needed now, and recommended action.
10. Run the final checks before completion.

## Output requirements

- Concise executive language
- Complete sentences
- Evidence or source reference for important factual claims
- Value stage for every reported result: reported, estimated, validated, or realized
- Owners and dates where available
- No invented metrics, status, causes, or commitments
- No external sending or publishing without approval

## Final checks

Before finalizing, verify that:

- The reporting period is correct.
- Reported, estimated, validated, and realized value are separated.
- Live or completed status is not presented as proof of business value.
- Every major claim is traceable to an approved source.
- Risks and blockers state the potential impact.
- Decisions needed are easy to find.
- Owners and dates are included where available.
- Missing or conflicting information is visible.
- The output follows the requested length and template.

## Human review

Pause for user review when the update contains:

- Sensitive personnel information
- Legal, compliance, security, or privacy concerns
- Unverified business results
- External communications
- Actions that alter data or send content to another system
